## 1. Thiết lập board

**Cột:** `Backlog` → `Todo` → `In Progress` → `In Review` → `Done`

**Label:** `Critical` (đỏ), `High` (cam), `Medium` (vàng), `Business Logic` (xanh dương), `Report` (xanh lá), `File I/O` (tím), `Docs` (xám)

**Mã thẻ:** `TASK-xx` đánh số theo thứ tự nên làm. Dùng để ghi phụ thuộc, đặt tên nhánh (`feat/task-06-...`) và ghi vào tiêu đề PR/commit.

**Cách tạo thẻ:** tiêu đề = `Title` (gồm cả mã), thành viên = `Assignee`, label và cột theo ghi chú, mô tả = `Description` (tiếng Anh, copy nguyên).

**Quy trình:** bắt đầu làm → `In Progress`; mở PR → `In Review`; merge → `Done`. Mỗi thẻ một nhánh theo `03.FLOW_USING_GIT.md`.

## 3. Thẻ cột `Todo`

### TASK-01 · Finalize the open business decisions

- **Assignee:** Tuấn
- **Labels:** Business Logic, Critical
- **Due:** 2026-10-23

**Description:**

Decide the rules below and write them at the end of `docs/04.BUSINESS_LOGIC_GAPS.md` (section 4), then announce them to the team:

1. Customers and products never used in a transaction can still be deleted; used ones cannot.
2. Orders cannot be dated in the future; past dates stay allowed.
3. Returns are per order line; returned items that are still sellable go back into stock.
4. A product with a past expiration date can still be saved, but a future production date is rejected.

### TASK-02 · Add order queries to check whether a customer or product already has orders

- **Assignee:** Tuấn
- **Labels:** Business Logic, Critical
- **Due:** 2026-10-22

**Description:**

In `OrderRepository` / `SalesService`, add: count the orders of a customer, check whether a product appears in any order (any status), and check whether a product is in a PENDING order. Do this first: TASK-06 and TASK-07 depend on it.

### TASK-03 · Create ID normalization helper

- **Assignee:** Tuấn
- **Labels:** Business Logic, Medium
- **Due:** 2026-10-28

**Description:**

Add a helper in `util` that trims and upper-cases IDs, so `p01` and `P01` are the same ID in lookups and duplicate checks. Use it in `InputHelper`. Do not enforce a fixed ID format.

### TASK-04 · Snapshot product name and unit on order lines

- **Assignee:** Tuấn
- **Labels:** Business Logic, High
- **Due:** 2026-10-28

**Description:**

When a product is added to an order, store its ID, name, unit and unit price on the `OrderDetail`. Make the order detail and history views read from this snapshot, so renaming a product later does not change old invoices.

### TASK-05 · Snapshot customer info and discount on confirmed orders

- **Assignee:** Tuấn
- **Labels:** Business Logic, High
- **Due:** 2026-10-28

**Description:**

When an order is confirmed, store the customer ID, name, membership type and discount rate on the `Order`. Make the views read from this snapshot, so changing a customer later does not change old invoices.

### TASK-06 · Prevent deleting customers who have transactions

- **Assignee:** Trung
- **Labels:** Business Logic, Critical
- **Due:** 2026-10-27

**Description:**

In `CustomerService.deleteCustomer`, refuse to delete a customer who has at least one transaction of any status, with the message "Customer C001 has N transactions and cannot be deleted." Customers with no transactions can still be deleted. Use the order queries from TASK-02.

### TASK-07 · Prevent deleting products that appear in transactions

- **Assignee:** Nhân
- **Labels:** Business Logic, Critical
- **Due:** 2026-10-27

**Description:**

In `ProductService.deleteProduct`, refuse to delete a product that appears in any order (any status, including PENDING), with a clear message. Products never sold can still be deleted. Use the order queries from TASK-02.

## 4. Thẻ cột `Backlog`

### TASK-08 · Add a stock change history log

- **Assignee:** Chánh
- **Labels:** Business Logic, High

**Description:**

Keep the current behavior (staff can still enter a new stock quantity, and stock still drops after a sale). Add a `StockMovement` log that records every stock change: product ID, old quantity, new quantity, source (manual update or sale), time and order ID if any. Add an Inventory menu option to view the log.

### TASK-09 · Reject future order dates

- **Assignee:** Tuấn
- **Labels:** Business Logic, Medium

**Description:**

Reject a transaction date later than today when creating an order. Past dates stay allowed. Check expiration against the day the sale is confirmed.

### TASK-10 · Add returns for completed orders

- **Assignee:** Tuấn
- **Labels:** Business Logic, High

**Description:**

Add a return option for COMPLETED orders: create a separate return record linked to the original order and leave the original unchanged. Require a reason. Return sellable items to stock through `InventoryService`. Do not allow returning more than was bought, or returning the same line twice. Start after TASK-04, TASK-05 and TASK-08.

### TASK-11 · Validate phone number and prevent duplicates

- **Assignee:** Trung
- **Labels:** Business Logic, Medium

**Description:**

Accept only valid Vietnamese phone numbers (10 digits starting with 0, or +84). Reject a phone number that already belongs to another customer, with the message "Phone number already belongs to customer C00x." Apply on both add and update (an update must not clash with the same customer).

### TASK-12 · Make customer IDs case-insensitive

- **Assignee:** Trung
- **Labels:** Business Logic, Medium

### TASK-13 · Reject future production dates when saving a product
